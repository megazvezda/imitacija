### Telemetry Sampling & Evaluation Matrix by Condition

| # | Disease / Condition | Primary Channels & Sensor Tier | Per Second (`Hz`) — Raw Acquisition | Per Minute (`1/min`) — Windowed / Derived | Per Hour (`1/hr`) — Clinical & Trend Cadence | Engineering & Physiological Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Orthostatic Intolerance / CV Deconditioning** | `heart_rate`, `hrv_rmssd`, `bp_systolic`, `bp_diastolic` *(Tier-1 PPG/Cuff)* + `hs_ctni` *(Tier-2 GFET event)* | **100 Hz** (raw PPG waveform for R-peak / IBI extraction) | **1 / min** (rolling 60s RMSSD, resting HR, Shock Index `HR/SBP`) | **1 / hr** (or **10 Hz** burst during active 3-min Stand Test) | Beat-to-beat intervals require 100 Hz PPG for accurate RMSSD (ESC/NASPE), while orthostatic drops (≥ 20 mmHg SBP) occur over 1–3 minutes of standing. |
| **2** | **Dehydration & Electrolyte Loss** | `sweat_rate`, `sweat_sodium` *(Tier-2 Hydrogel)* + `body_mass`, `urine_specific_gravity`, `fluid_intake` *(Prompted)* | **1 Hz** (during active EVA/exercise) / **0 Hz** (at rest) | **1 / min** (integrated sweat volume ΔV_fluid & Na⁺ loss rate) | **1 / hr** (hydration balance check; daily pre/post mass & USG) | Sweat flux and Na⁺ conductivity shift continuously over minutes during EVA exertion; resting hydration is governed by hourly/daily intake and mass balance. |
| **3** | **Acute Infection / Immune Dysregulation** | `zhf_core_temp`, `heart_rate`, `respiratory_rate`, `spo2`, `bp_systolic` *(Tier-1)* + `latent_virus_shedding` *(Saliva)* + `il6` *(Tier-2 GFET)* | **1 Hz** (ZHF thermal equilibrium & pulse oximetry) | **1 / min** (respiratory rate & NEWS2 / qSOFA / SIRS composite score) | **1 / hr** (NEWS2 trend evaluation; daily/48h saliva viral shedding & gated IL-6) | Core temperature and systemic infection evolve over hours, but NEWS2/qSOFA vital signs must be evaluated minute-by-minute to catch acute sepsis deterioration early. |
| **4** | **Hypoxia & CO₂ Exposure** | `cabin_co2` *(Tier-1 SCD40)*, `spo2`, `respiratory_rate`, `heart_rate` *(Tier-1)* | **1 Hz** (SCD40 ppCO₂ & SpO₂ fast-response loop) | **12 / min** (every 5s acute ppCO₂ & SpO₂ < 94% alarm check) | **1 / hr** (ambient scrubber drift & chronic headache exposure log) | Cabin CO₂ spikes and hypoxemia are acute life-support emergencies requiring sub-minute detection (5s polling) so the crew can check scrubbers or relocate immediately. |
| **5** | **Sleep Deficiency & Circadian Misalignment** | `activity_counts`, `light_exposure`, `hrv_rmssd`, `zhf_core_temp` *(Tier-1)* + `eeg_theta_alpha` *(Tier-2 GFET)* + `pvt_reaction_time` | **250 Hz** (during active EEG/PVT blocks) / **1 Hz** (actigraphy & lux) | **1 / min** (epoch activity counts, lux dose, and CFE Θ/α ratio) | **1 / hr** (circadian core-temp nadir tracking; 1×/day sleep & 5-min PVT) | EEG spectral FFT (4–13 Hz) needs ≥ 250 Hz sampling during active monitoring, whereas circadian phase shifts and sleep debt accumulate over hours and days. |
| **6** | **Behavioral Health Decline** | `salivary_cortisol_slope` *(Tier-2 GFET)*, `mood_score` *(PHQ-9/GAD-7/EMA)*, `pvt_reaction_time`, `sleep_duration` | **0 Hz** (no continuous second-by-second sampling needed) | **0 / min** (event-driven only upon scheduled assay or EMA prompt) | **0.25 / hr** (every 4 hrs for waking diurnal cortisol slope; 1×/day EMA/PVT) | Psychological state and HPA-axis cortisol follow circadian diurnal curves; high-frequency polling creates false alarms from normal ultradian hormone pulses. |
| **7** | **Negative Energy Balance & Nutrition** | `glucose` *(Tier-1 CGM)*, `body_mass` *(SLAMMD)*, `fluid_intake`, `vitamin_d_25oh` | **0.2 Hz** (every 5s interstitial sensor read) | **0.2 / min** (every 5 min CGM glucose value & meal-window tag) | **1 / hr** (caloric/glycemic trend; weekly body-mass slope; monthly 25-OH-D) | Interstitial glucose equilibrates over 5–10 minutes; weekly body-mass slope tracks chronic caloric deficit without noise from daily fluid fluctuations. |
| **8** | **Bone Demineralization & Renal Stone Risk** | `urinary_ntx_pct_baseline` *(Tier-2 GFET)*, `urine_calcium_volume`, `exercise_compliance` *(Tier-2 Hydrogel)*, `fluid_intake` | **50 Hz** (ARED exercise kinematic load asymmetry via hydrogel strips) | **1 / min** (cumulative ARED work/rep asymmetry & hydration pace) | **0.04 / hr** (24-hr urine volume < 2.0 L/day; weekly/monthly NTx % change) | ARED joint mechanics require 50 Hz capture during workouts to catch form failure, whereas bone turnover (NTx) and renal stone supersaturation are slow metabolic processes. |
| **9** | **SANS / Cephalic Fluid Shift** | `cephalic_fluid_index` *(Tier-2 Jugular Hydrogel)* + `visual_acuity`, `amsler_grid` *(Software Screen)* | **50 Hz** (jugular venous pulse waveform & micro-strain ΔR/R₀) | **1 / min** (mean cephalic strain % & estimated ICP trend) | **1 / hr** (sustained shift check; weekly LogMAR & Amsler screen if triggered) | Jugular pulse waveforms oscillate at cardiac frequencies (~1–2 Hz, requiring 50 Hz capture), while optic disc edema develops over days to weeks of sustained cephalic pooling. |
| **10** | **Radiation Dose & Solar Particle Event (SPE)** | `dose_rate`, `radiation_dose` *(Tier-1 Dosimeter)*, `spe_flag` *(External Telemetry)* | **1 Hz** (radiation dose rate µSv/h & SPE boolean interrupt) | **1 / min** (rolling dose-rate integration & storm-shelter alert check) | **1 / hr** (cumulative mission mSv vs. 600 mSv career limit) | Solar Particle Events can escalate dose rates within minutes; 1 Hz dosimetry ensures an immediate *"Get to shelter"* alarm while hourly/daily logs track career limits. |
